01 / About

The engine room for local business.

Big chains have tech teams, apps and custom software. The shop down the road, in any city, has a billing machine and a WhatsApp group. Postly Engines exists to close that gap.

Our story

We started by walking into local shops that were busy every night and still could not say where the money went.

Orders on paper. Stock that vanished. Owners stuck at the counter because they could not see anything from home. Every owner had the same problem: their business had no engine. So we decided to build one, around each business, and to stay with them while it runs.
